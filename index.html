About Brian

Minneapolis, MN

I design the systems that let a brand speak in one voice, whoever is holding the pen.

I'm a Minneapolis designer with more than twelve years across agency and in-house teams. I started in production at Red Circle Agency, then moved through Sportsdigita and Schermer into in-house work, where I learned to own a brand end to end.

At HidrateSpark I was the first designer hired, and grew into Senior Designer while designing the app, the packaging and the store. At Irrigreen I partnered with the CMO to build the brand and a design system in Claude Design that sales, marketing, support and engineering all use on their own.

When it helps, I bring AI into the process to explore directions faster. The finished work still comes together by hand in Figma and Adobe Creative Suite, where brand fit and quality are my call.
